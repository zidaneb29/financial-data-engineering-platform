# Public preview data notices

The MIT license in the repository root applies to project-authored software and documentation. The two factual extracts in this directory have separate source and reuse terms.

- `treasury-10y.csv` comes directly from the U.S. Department of the Treasury’s Daily Treasury Par Yield Curve Rates archive. Treasury lists the dataset under CC0 1.0. [Treasury archive](https://home.treasury.gov/interest-rates-data-csv-archive/) · [Federal catalog and license](https://catalog.data.gov/dataset/interest-rate-statistics-daily-treasury-yield-curve-rates)
- `microsoft-revenue.csv` contains numerical facts from Microsoft Corporation’s public Forms 10-K. Each row identifies its EDGAR filing. The SEC permits reuse of public EDGAR filing content. [SEC reuse policy](https://www.sec.gov/about/webmaster-frequently-asked-questions)
